## 📈 Lessons from Etsy research
- Competitors sell cheap (€2–10), often AI-made ebooks. I won't compete on quantity
- A permanent small discount ("25% off") helps listings stand out in search
- Seasonal versions work (e.g., "Winter work lunches")
- Review ratio: roughly 1 review per 20 sales
- Avoid health claims ("detox", "natural healing")

## ✍️ Writing in English: notes to self
- Give both units: *200 g (1 cup) rice*, *200 °C (400 °F)*
- US vs UK names differ: *ground meat* (US) / *mince* (UK), *bell pepper* (US) / *pepper* (UK). Pick one and stay consistent
- Prefer ingredients that are easy to find everywhere (rice, chicken, tortillas)
