3 rules for a lunch that stays fresh

1
Sauce on the side
Pack it in a small separate container so your grains don't get soggy.
2
Greens on top
Keep lettuce and fresh herbs away from wet ingredients. Add them last.
3
Keep it cool
Fridge until you leave, then a fridge or ice pack at work. Prepped lunches keep 3–4 days.
🥪
Bonus: the sandwich toaster hack
Only have a sandwich toaster at work? Fill a tortilla with your protein, veggies and a little cheese, fold it and toast it. Warm, crispy quesadilla in 3 minutes.
